# Gaming Backlog Organizer

A personal app for organizing a gaming backlog across platforms and stores.

## Language

**Backlog**:
The collection of Games the owner has not finished playing yet.
_Avoid_: Library, collection, wishlist

**Game**:
A single title the owner tracks, independent of where it is owned. One Game is one entry in the Backlog, even if it is owned on several platforms.
_Avoid_: Entry, item, title

**Copy**:
One way the owner holds a Game: a specific platform and store (e.g. Hades on Steam, Hades on Switch). A Game has one or more Copies.
_Avoid_: Edition, version, ownership, instance
